About Me

Professional Profile

I am a dedicated microbiologist and epidemiologist with over 12 years of experience in infectious disease research and public health data analysis. My work integrates traditional laboratory techniques with modern computational approaches to understand disease dynamics and develop effective interventions.

Currently leading research initiatives at the Center for Infectious Disease Research, focusing on antimicrobial resistance patterns and emerging pathogen surveillance in Sub-Saharan Africa.
